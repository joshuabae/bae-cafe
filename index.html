About the Artists

Josh Bae

Josh's love for espresso began with the purchase of his espresso machine. He has spent significant time sharing his passion for coffee and matcha with his community. A true matcha lover long before it became popular in mainstream culture, Josh is dedicated to bringing people together through unique and memorable café experiences.

Bryan Chen

Bryan is a passionate tea enthusiast who delights in exploring the world of leaves, flavors, and traditions. While he enjoys the occasional long black, his heart belongs to tea. Bryan dreams of one day raising his own tea farm, sharing the beauty and culture of tea with future generations.
